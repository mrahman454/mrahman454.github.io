About

I am a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, working on symmetric-key cryptography. Before joining NTU, I was a Program-Specific Researcher at Kyoto University and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Hyogo, Japan. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, with a thesis on cryptanalysis of symmetric-key schemes using classical and quantum techniques.

Research

Cipher design

Low-latency, high-throughput and post-quantum block ciphers, PRFs and authenticated encryption.

Cryptanalysis

Yoyo, boomerang, differential, division-property and fault attacks, plus automated tools with MILP and SAT.

Quantum cryptanalysis

Grover and Simon based attacks and quantum resource estimation for symmetric-key primitives.

Real-world protocols

Key commitment of AEAD schemes and the security of symmetric-key primitives inside deployed protocols.
