Teaching approach

In Computing Systems Security, I organised the course to move from foundational concepts to advanced topics, including hardware attacks (Meltdown, Spectre), network attacks and SQL injection. Students worked on hands-on assignments simulating real-world attacks and secure implementations, and on research projects where they studied recent papers and presented their findings.
